# The Eight Fighters of Ashblade

Ashblade has eight fighters. Kael is free from the start, and each of the others unlocks through a mission. The Dev is the secret eighth, who is also the final boss on wave 50.

## Rules every fighter shares

**Controls.** Click (or hold) attacks, Q and E use the two skills, and R fires the ultimate once its meter is full. Q and E cost energy, which basic hits refill. The ultimate meter fills only from basic-attack damage.

**Charged attacks.** Kael, Rin, Rivet and Grom fight in melee. For them, holding the attack winds up a charged move instead of swinging: half a charge gives half the effect.

**Flash Strike.** The first sword swing or skill within 0.6 seconds after a dash does 2.5 times the damage.

**Awakening.** Each upgrade has an evolved version. Complete any 5 evolutions in a run and your fighter awakens: a ring of light, a beam, and the new look. Awakening also gives every fighter:

- basic hits and the ultimate 30% stronger
- Q and E a quarter cheaper and a fifth faster to recharge
- the ultimate charging a quarter faster, and keeping 30% of its charge after each use
- new colors for the basic attack, the charged attack, the skills and the ultimate, drawn thicker
- one extra point of maximum health

Each fighter's own awakened powers are listed in their section below.

**Their look.** All eight are chibi pixel art about 54 pixels tall (Grom 58), with a dark outline all round. They breathe, blink and let their scarves, capes and hair move in the wind when they stand, step with each foot when they walk and run, and fall over when they die. Each weapon is its own sprite held in the hand and turned to wherever the player aims: Kael's longsword, Vega's revolver (a minigun during Last Stand), Morrow's orb staff, Rin's katana, Rivet's wrench, Grom's war hammer and Unit-7's arm cannon. The Dev types on his laptop instead. A swing sweeps the blade through the cut with afterimages, a shot kicks the gun back, Q and E twirl a blade once, and R lifts the weapon high. The awakened look has its own colors, and the weapon changes with it.

**Silver and gold frames.** On the select screen, a fighter you've cleared wave 25 with gets a silver frame, and one you've cleared wave 50 with gets a gold frame with a crown.

## Kael, the Blade

**Unlock:** free from the start · **Awakened form:** Ashen Kael

> The last guard of the pit. His blade holds the final ember of the old sun.

### Look
Spiky violet hair over dark, determined eyes, an ember-orange scarf wrapped high, and a dark violet tunic with a gold belt buckle. His Ember Blade glows in every swing, leaving a burning arc.

Awakened as **Ashen Kael**, his hair and clothes turn silver-grey and his scarf turns gold.

### How he plays
The all-rounder and the first fighter you get. Quick on his feet, solid in a fight, short on reach.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 3 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Sword Combo | Four hits: slash, X-cut, thrust, whirlwind |
| Hold click | Ashen Crescent | A wide burning cut that also throws a wave of fire ahead |
| Q (30 energy) | Rift | Blinks forward and cuts every enemy on the line |
| E (45 energy) | Tempest | Knocks enemies back and chains lightning between them |
| R (ultimate) | Thousand Cuts | Stops time and cuts every enemy on screen |

**Awakened powers:** his cuts set enemies on fire, his skills throw a ring of fire, and Thousand Cuts leaves the whole screen burning. His Ashen Crescent fans out four more burning crescents, and a line of white fire erupts along the cut.

### Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies catch fire, and embers and ash rise off the body.
- **His death:** he drops to one knee, plants his sword and crumbles to ash from the head down. The sword stays, its flame going out. *The last ember goes out.*

## Vega Hale, the Gunner

**Unlock:** reach wave 10 · **Awakened form:** Deadeye Vega

> A bounty hunter who followed one bounty too deep into the pit.

### Look
A wide brown cowboy hat with a gold band, long brown hair, and a red bandana over the lower half of the face. A long brown duster coat and a revolver that fires with a flash and a puff of smoke.

Awakened as **Deadeye Vega**, the hat and coat turn charcoal and the bandana deep crimson.

### How she plays
The longest reach in the game. Hold to shoot, and reloading is automatic. She is fragile, so keep your distance.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Revolver | Fires while held, reloads after 14 shots |
| Q (25 energy) | Buckshot | An 11-pellet shotgun blast at point-blank range that pushes her back |
| E (35 energy) | Deadeye | Crosshairs lock onto the 6 nearest enemies, then she fans the hammer: one sure critical shot each. No aiming needed |
| R (ultimate) | Last Stand | Four seconds of minigun |

**Awakened powers:** her shots ricochet to the next enemy, her skills fire a ring of 10 gold rounds, and Last Stand opens with a homing volley at up to 10 enemies.

### Kill and death
- **Her kills:** head shots. Blood jets up, gun smoke drifts, a spent casing drops.
- **Her death:** she spins, falls flat on her back, and her dropped revolver fires one last shot into the sky. *One bullet short.*

## Morrow, the Wizard

**Unlock:** land a 100-hit combo · **Awakened form:** Archmage Morrow

> A warlock who traded his face for the names of the stars.

### Look
A deep purple hood trimmed in gold, with nothing inside but shadow and two glowing blue eyes, and a long white beard spilling over purple robes. He casts with an arcane staff topped by a blue orb, and swirls of violet magic ring him when he casts.

Awakened as **Archmage Morrow**, his hood and robes turn white-lavender with gold trim and his eyes burn pink.

### How he plays
The hardest hitter and the frailest fighter. Every spell is strong, but he can't take many hits.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 1 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Arcane Bolt | Homing magic bolts |
| Q (25 energy) | Arcane Missiles | A rune circle flares at his feet and 5 violet missiles leave his staff one after another, each hunting its own target |
| E (40 energy) | Blizzard | A frost storm on the aimed spot for 3 seconds: ice rains inside a rune circle, cutting and freezing everything in it |
| R (ultimate) | Starfall | Meteors land on every enemy for about 3 seconds |

**Awakened powers:** his bolts arc to a second enemy, his skills call lightning on the 3 nearest enemies, and Starfall adds lightning on 8 more.

### Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies unravel into motes of arcane light.
- **His death:** he rises as his last spell turns on him and implodes. His hat falls where he stood. *The spell was never finished.*

## Rin, the Samurai

**Unlock:** make 25 perfect dodges in one run · **Awakened form:** Oni Rin

> The last samurai of the Red Oni clan. Still has homework due Friday.

### Look
Bright cyan hair fading to pink at the tips, tied with pink ribbons, big violet eyes, a white sailor top with a red ribbon, a red sash and a navy skirt. Her katana cuts in pink arcs and cherry petals, and pink afterimages trail behind her when she runs.

Awakened as **Oni Rin**, her hair turns violet, her reds and pinks turn hot pink and her skirt deep purple. Her sword arcs, cuts and petals turn gold and draw much thicker.

### How she plays
The fastest fighter, with two dashes. Her reach is short, so she fights up close and sends enemy shots back at them.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Katana | Four hits: cut, X-cut, lunging stab, and a quick-draw that dashes through and cuts twice |
| Dash, then attack | Flash Strike | Dash into Senko for her biggest hit |
| Hold click | Iai Dash | Hand on the hilt, she flash-steps through everything ahead, untouchable, and the cuts open a beat later in a burst of petals. Every shot on her path is parried back |
| Q (50 energy) | Senko | She slashes and a beam of light shoots out of the blade across the arena in an instant: huge damage to every enemy on the line, every enemy shot on it destroyed, and the recoil pushes her back a step |
| E (30 energy) | Hana Arashi | For 4 seconds a ring of petal blades spins around her. It cuts anything that comes close, parries every shot that reaches it, and she moves 25% faster |
| R (ultimate) | Issen | Time stops while she flickers from enemy to enemy in 11 cuts, then she sheathes her sword and everything she cut bursts |

**Awakened powers:** a petal cut opens on every enemy a beat after her blade, her skills cut a ring that parries every shot near her, and after Issen the blossom storm rises around her for free. Her charged Iai Dash opens a second, wider cut along her path a moment later, ending in a burst of petals.

### Kill and death
- **Her kills:** a burst of petals, and the cut line hangs in the air.
- **Her death:** a storm of cherry blossoms rises around her and thickens until she fades behind it, then she scatters into petals and the wind carries them away. *She scatters with the blossoms.*

## Rivet, the Mechanic

**Unlock:** kill 1500 enemies in total, across all runs · **Awakened form:** Rivet Mk-II

> A salvager who builds her weapons from what the Colossus left behind.

### Look
A dark cap with brass goggles and cyan lenses, messy brown hair, an orange scarf and a work jumpsuit. She swings a big mechanic's wrench that sparks gold. She arrives by breaking out of a supply crate.

Awakened as **Rivet Mk-II**, her orange gear turns steel blue and her goggles keep their cyan glow.

### How she plays
Balanced in every stat, and she never fights alone: turrets and coils do half the work.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Wrench | Three hits: swing, twirl, ground slam |
| Hold click | Overclock Slam | The wrench smashes the floor ahead and stuns. From half a charge up, lightning arcs to nearby enemies |
| Q (30 energy) | Turret | Builds an auto-gun that lasts 14 seconds, up to 2 at once |
| E (40 energy) | Tesla Coil | Zaps 3 enemies at a time for 7 seconds |
| R (ultimate) | Iron Titan | A giant mech rockets down onto the arena, stomps after enemies, throws heavy piston punches, fires salvos of 6 homing missiles, and ground-pounds every 4 seconds to stun everything near, for 18 seconds (30 with its evolution), then jets away |

**Awakened powers:** every third hit arcs to two more enemies, her skills release a tesla chain through 5 enemies, and Iron Titan lands with a 10-enemy chain and lightning strikes. Her charged Overclock Slam arcs lightning through more enemies and calls strikes down on the nearest.

### Kill and death
- **Her kills:** enemies come apart into bolts, nuts and springs.
- **Her death:** her suit overloads, beeping faster and faster, then blows apart. Her goggles bounce away. *Warranty void.*

## Grom, the Tank

**Unlock:** beat any boss without getting hit · **Awakened form:** Titan Grom

> The last knight of the King's Guard. He came back to end what he served.

### Look
A knight in steel-grey plate with gold trim, a closed helm whose visor burns orange from within, and a red cape. He swings a war hammer that leaves golden arcs, and enters by falling from the sky and cracking the floor.

Awakened as **Titan Grom**, his armor turns near black with blood-red trim, his visor still burning, and his effects turn blood red.

### How he plays
Slow and nearly unbreakable. The most health in the game and the heaviest hits.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 5 / 5 | 1 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 5 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | War Hammer | Three slow hits: sweep, ground slam, full spin |
| Hold click | Earthshatter | The hammer cracks the ground in a wide ring that stuns everything in it. The fuller the charge, the wider the ring |
| Q (25 energy) | Bulldoze | Charges forward and pins enemies against walls |
| E (40 energy) | Ground Slam | Shockwaves that stun |
| R (ultimate) | Fortress | Six seconds of no damage, and shots bounce back, with a pulsing glow round him |

**Awakened powers:** every blow sends a shockwave through the enemies around the target, his skills quake and stun, and Fortress opens with a great quake that stuns everything near. His charged Earthshatter sends red aftershock quakes rolling out after the slam.

### Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies are flattened into a wide splat and a ring of dust.
- **His death:** his armor cracks plate by plate and he falls forward like a wall. The ground shakes and his helmet rolls away. *The wall has fallen.*

## Unit-7, the Robot

**Unlock:** defeat the Ashen King · **Awakened form:** Unit-7 Omega

> A war machine that woke up with one order left: stop the King.

### Look
A dark navy war drone on heavy treads, with two red-tipped antennas and a screen for a face that shows a red cross. Cyan lights glow along its body, and a pulse cannon fires bright blue shots. It glides instead of walking, and arrives by teleporting down in a beam.

Awakened as **Unit-7 Omega**, its lights and screen glow pink, and it hovers on twin jets instead of rolling.

### How it plays
A ranged fighter built around one long laser. Hold too long and it overheats.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 4 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 3 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Laser Beam | A continuous laser. Watch the heat, or it overheats |
| Q (30 energy) | Rocket Salvo | Six homing rockets |
| E (35 energy) | Barrier | Blocks 3 hits, then bursts |
| R (ultimate) | Orbital Laser | A beam from the sky follows your aim for 4.5 seconds |

**Awakened powers:** its laser scorches what it touches, its skills launch 3 homing mini-rockets, and the Orbital Laser comes with a ring of 8 homing rockets.

### Kill and death
- **Its kills:** the laser disintegrates enemies into white-hot pixels and ash.
- **Its death:** it shorts out with sparks and ERR, its head pops off on a spring, and the red eye fades to dark. *Shutdown complete.*

## The Dev

**Unlock:** reach wave 50 · **Awakened form:** Root

> The one who wrote this world. Now debugging it from the inside.

### Look
A programmer with messy brown hair and black shades in a blue hoodie, typing on a laptop in a motorized wheelchair, with holo screens glowing cyan at his sides. Since he rides, he glides instead of walking. He is also the final boss on wave 50, where he fights you in his hooded form.

Awakened as **Root**, his hoodie turns black, and his screens and wheels glow neon green.

### How he plays
He starts weaker than everyone else and becomes the strongest fighter in the game. He has 6 health and one shot at a time at first, and grows stronger with every wave and every 20 kills. He's about even by wave 15, then ramps hard after wave 20: about 1.7x power by wave 20, 3.2x by wave 30 and 7.5x by wave 50.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 4 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

On the select screen his stat bars glitch.

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Ping | Shots that plant bugs. Enough bugs crash an enemy: five early, four from wave 15, three from wave 30. His spread widens past waves 20 and 35 |
| Q (30 energy) | Exploit | Marks a group of enemies and hacks them all |
| E (40 energy) | Firewall | Deletes bullets and fries enemies |
| R (ultimate) | rm -rf | Deletes everything on screen |

**Awakened powers:** each hit forks a bug into a nearby enemy, his skills crash the 3 nearest enemies, and his ultimate glitches everything in range.

### Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies are deleted into glitch pixels, now and then with a 404.
- **His death:** the screen tears, a FATAL ERROR box pops up, and he is deleted line by line. His empty wheelchair rolls away. *Process terminated.*
