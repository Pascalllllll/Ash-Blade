# Unit-7, the Robot

**Class:** Robot · **Unlock:** defeat the Ashen King · **Awakened form:** Unit-7 Omega

> A war machine that woke up with one order left: stop the King.

## Look
A grey war machine with a boxy head, two red-tipped antennas, and a black visor where a single red eye glows. A cyan core shines in its chest. It rolls on tank treads over three big road wheels that turn as it drives, so it glides instead of walking. It arrives by teleporting down in a beam.

Awakened as **Unit-7 Omega**, its body turns black, its eye and antennas go gold, and its wheels turn gold with pink hubs.

Awakened, its laser scorches what it touches, its powers launch 3 homing mini-rockets, and the Orbital Laser comes with a ring of 8 homing rockets. Awakening also makes basic hits and the ultimate 30% stronger, Q and E a quarter cheaper and a fifth faster to recharge, and the ultimate charge a quarter faster, keeping 30% of its charge after each use.

## How it plays
A ranged fighter built around one long laser. Hold too long and it overheats.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 4 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 3 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Laser Beam | A continuous laser. Watch the heat, or it overheats |
| Q (30 energy) | Rocket Salvo | Six homing rockets |
| E (35 energy) | Barrier | Blocks 3 hits, then bursts |
| R (ultimate) | Orbital Laser | A beam from the sky follows your aim for 4.5 seconds |

## Kill and death
- **Its kills:** the laser disintegrates enemies into white-hot pixels and ash.
- **Its death:** it shorts out with sparks and ERR, its head pops off on a spring, and the red eye fades to dark. *Shutdown complete.*
