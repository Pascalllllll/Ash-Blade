# The Dev

**Unlock:** reach wave 50 · **Awakened form:** Root

The rules every fighter shares (controls, charged attacks, Flash Strike, awakening and its bonuses) are in [heroes.md](heroes.md).

> The one who wrote this world. Now debugging it from the inside.

## Look
A programmer with messy brown hair and black shades in a blue hoodie, typing on a laptop in a motorized wheelchair, with holo screens glowing cyan at his sides. Since he rides, he glides instead of walking. He is also the final boss on wave 50, where he fights you in his hooded form.

Awakened as **Root**, his hoodie turns black, and his screens and wheels glow neon green.

## How he plays
He starts weaker than everyone else and becomes the strongest fighter in the game. He has 6 health and one shot at a time at first, and grows stronger with every wave and every 20 kills. He's about even by wave 15, then ramps hard after wave 20: about 1.7x power by wave 20, 3.2x by wave 30 and 7.5x by wave 50.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 4 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

On the select screen his stat bars glitch.

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Ping | Shots that plant bugs. Enough bugs crash an enemy: five early, four from wave 15, three from wave 30. His spread widens past waves 20 and 35 |
| Q (30 energy) | Exploit | Marks a group of enemies and hacks them all |
| E (40 energy) | Firewall | Deletes bullets and fries enemies |
| R (ultimate) | rm -rf | Deletes everything on screen |

**Awakened powers:** each hit forks a bug into a nearby enemy, his skills crash the 3 nearest enemies, and his ultimate glitches everything in range.

## Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies are deleted into glitch pixels, now and then with a 404.
- **His death:** the screen tears, a FATAL ERROR box pops up, and he is deleted line by line. His empty wheelchair rolls away. *Process terminated.*
