# Rivet, the Mechanic

**Class:** Mechanic · **Unlock:** kill 1500 enemies in total, across all runs · **Awakened form:** Rivet Mk-II

> A salvager who builds her weapons from what the Colossus left behind.

## Look
A grey cap with welding goggles on the band, brown hair, a white tank top and an orange jumpsuit. She carries a wrench nearly as long as she is tall. She arrives by breaking out of a supply crate.

Awakened as **Rivet Mk-II**, her cap turns gold and her goggles glow cyan.

Awakened, every third hit arcs to two more enemies, her powers release a tesla chain through 5 enemies, and Iron Titan lands with a 10-enemy chain and lightning strikes. Awakening also makes basic hits and the ultimate 30% stronger, Q and E a quarter cheaper and a fifth faster to recharge, and the ultimate charge a quarter faster, keeping 30% of its charge after each use. Her charged Overclock Slam arcs lightning through more enemies and calls strikes down on the nearest.

## How she plays
Balanced in every stat, and she never fights alone: turrets and coils do half the work.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Wrench | Three hits: swing, twirl, ground slam |
| Hold click | Overclock Slam | The wrench smashes the floor ahead and stuns. From half a charge up, lightning arcs to nearby enemies |
| Q (30 energy) | Turret | Builds an auto-gun that lasts 14 seconds, up to 2 at once |
| E (40 energy) | Tesla Coil | Zaps 3 enemies at a time for 7 seconds |
| R (ultimate) | Iron Titan | A giant mech rockets down onto the arena, stomps after enemies, throws heavy piston punches, fires salvos of 6 homing missiles, and ground-pounds every 4 seconds to stun everything near, for 18 seconds (30 with its evolution), then jets away |

## Kill and death
- **Her kills:** enemies come apart into bolts, nuts and springs.
- **Her death:** her suit overloads, beeping faster and faster, then blows apart. Her goggles bounce away. *Warranty void.*
