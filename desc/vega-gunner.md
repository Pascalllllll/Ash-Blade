# Vega Hale, the Gunner

**Unlock:** reach wave 10 · **Awakened form:** Deadeye Vega

The rules every fighter shares (controls, charged attacks, Flash Strike, awakening and its bonuses) are in [heroes.md](heroes.md).

> A bounty hunter who followed one bounty too deep into the pit.

## Look
A wide brown cowboy hat with a gold band, long brown hair, and a red bandana over the lower half of the face. A long brown duster coat and a revolver that fires with a flash and a puff of smoke.

Awakened as **Deadeye Vega**, the hat and coat turn charcoal and the bandana deep crimson.

## How she plays
The longest reach in the game. Hold to shoot, and reloading is automatic. She is fragile, so keep your distance.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Revolver | Fires while held, reloads after 14 shots |
| Q (25 energy) | Buckshot | An 11-pellet shotgun blast at point-blank range that pushes her back |
| E (35 energy) | Deadeye | Crosshairs lock onto the 6 nearest enemies, then she fans the hammer: one sure critical shot each. No aiming needed |
| R (ultimate) | Last Stand | Four seconds of minigun |

**Awakened powers:** her shots ricochet to the next enemy, her skills fire a ring of 10 gold rounds, and Last Stand opens with a homing volley at up to 10 enemies.

## Kill and death
- **Her kills:** head shots. Blood jets up, gun smoke drifts, a spent casing drops.
- **Her death:** she spins, falls flat on her back, and her dropped revolver fires one last shot into the sky. *One bullet short.*
