# Vega Hale, the Gunner

**Class:** Gunner · **Unlock:** reach wave 10 · **Awakened form:** Deadeye Vega

> A bounty hunter who followed one bounty too deep into the pit.

## Look
A wide-brimmed hat with a gold band, long brown hair, and a red bandana over the lower half of her face. A long brown duster coat over a cream shirt, a gunbelt, boots, and a revolver she never seems to reload by hand.

Awakened as **Deadeye Vega**, her hat turns pale with a gold band, her bandana goes black and her coat dark red.

## How she plays
The longest reach in the game. Hold to shoot, and reloading is automatic. She is fragile, so keep your distance.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hold click | Revolver | Fires while held, reloads after 14 shots |
| Q (25 energy) | Buckshot | An 11-pellet shotgun blast at point-blank range that pushes you back |
| E (35 energy) | Deadeye | Time slows and crosshairs lock onto the 6 nearest enemies, then she fans the hammer: one sure critical shot each. No aiming needed |
| R (ultimate) | Last Stand | Four seconds of minigun |

## Kill and death
- **Her kills:** head shots. Blood jets up, gun smoke drifts, a spent casing drops.
- **Her death:** she spins, falls flat on her back, and her dropped revolver fires one last shot into the sky. *One bullet short.*
