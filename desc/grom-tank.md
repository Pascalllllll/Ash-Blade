# Grom, the Tank

**Class:** Tank · **Unlock:** beat any boss without getting hit · **Awakened form:** Titan Grom

> The last knight of the King's Guard. He came back to end what he served.

## Look
A knight in full blue-grey plate with gold trim, a closed helm whose visor glows orange from within, and a crimson cape that snaps behind him. He swings a war hammer bigger than most enemies. He enters by falling from the sky and cracking the floor.

Awakened as **Titan Grom**, his armor turns gold and his visor glows red.

## How he plays
Slow and nearly unbreakable. The most health in the game and the heaviest hits.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 5 / 5 | 1 / 5 | 3 / 5 | 5 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | War Hammer | Three slow hits: sweep, ground slam, full spin |
| Hold click | Earthshatter | The hammer cracks the ground in a wide ring that stuns everything in it. The fuller the charge, the wider the ring |
| Q (25 energy) | Bulldoze | Charges forward and pins enemies against walls |
| E (40 energy) | Ground Slam | Shockwaves that stun |
| R (ultimate) | Fortress | Six seconds of no damage, and shots bounce back |

## Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies are flattened into a wide splat and a ring of dust.
- **His death:** his armor cracks plate by plate and he falls forward like a wall. The ground shakes and his helmet rolls away. *The wall has fallen.*
