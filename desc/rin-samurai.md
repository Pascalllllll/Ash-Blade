# Rin, the Samurai

**Class:** Samurai · **Unlock:** make 25 perfect dodges in one run · **Awakened form:** Oni Rin

> The last samurai of the Red Oni clan. Still has homework due Friday.

## Look
A schoolgirl with side-swept teal hair that fades to violet and pink at the tips, and big violet eyes. She wears a white sailor uniform with a red ribbon, a red jacket tied around her hips, a short pleated navy skirt, white knee socks and small shoes. Her katana rides at her hip. She runs like a ninja, leaning into every step with pink afterimages trailing behind her.

Awakened as **Oni Rin**, she keeps her hairstyle in purple and pink: violet hair with pink tips and a gold pin, pink eyes, and a dark purple uniform with a pink ribbon, a magenta jacket and pink shoes. Her sword arcs, cuts, petals and powers turn gold and draw much thicker.

Awakened, a petal cut opens on every enemy a beat after her blade, her powers cut a ring that parries every shot near her, and after Issen the blossom storm rises around her for free. Awakening also makes basic hits and the ultimate 30% stronger, Q and E a quarter cheaper and a fifth faster to recharge, and the ultimate charge a quarter faster, keeping 30% of its charge after each use. Her charged Iai Dash opens a second, wider cut along her path a moment later, ending in a burst of gold petals.

## How she plays
The fastest fighter, with two dashes. Her reach is short, so she fights up close and sends enemy shots back at them.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Katana | Four hits: cut, X-cut, lunging stab, and a quick-draw that dashes through and cuts twice |
| Dash, then attack | Flash Strike | The first sword swing or skill within 0.6 seconds after a dash does 2.5 times the damage. Dash into Senko for her biggest hit |
| Hold click | Iai Dash | Hand on the hilt, she flash-steps through everything ahead, untouchable, and the cuts open a beat later in a burst of petals. Every shot on her path is parried back. Half a charge, half the dash |
| Q (50 energy) | Senko | She slashes and a beam of light shoots out of the blade across the arena in an instant: huge damage to every enemy on the line, every enemy shot on it destroyed, and the recoil pushes her back a step |
| E (30 energy) | Hana Arashi | For 4 seconds a ring of petal blades spins around her. It cuts anything that comes close, parries every shot that reaches it, and she moves 25% faster |
| R (ultimate) | Issen | Time stops while she flickers from enemy to enemy in 11 cuts, then she sheathes her sword and everything she cut bursts |

## Kill and death
- **Her kills:** a burst of petals, and the cut line hangs in the air.
- **Her death:** a storm of cherry blossoms rises around her and thickens until she fades behind it, then she scatters into petals and the wind carries them away. *She scatters with the blossoms.*
