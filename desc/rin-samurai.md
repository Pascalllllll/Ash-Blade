# Rin, the Samurai

**Class:** Samurai · **Unlock:** make 25 perfect dodges in one run · **Awakened form:** Oni Rin

> The last samurai of the Red Oni clan. Still has homework due Friday.

## Look
A schoolgirl with side-swept teal hair that fades to violet and pink at the tips, and big violet eyes. She wears a white sailor uniform with a red ribbon, a red jacket tied around her hips, a short pleated navy skirt, white knee socks and small shoes. Her katana rides at her hip. She runs like a ninja, leaning into every step with pink afterimages trailing behind her.

Awakened as **Oni Rin**, her hair turns white, two small red oni horns appear, her eyes glow red and her uniform turns black with dark stockings.

## How she plays
The fastest fighter, with two dashes. Short reach and little health, so she lives by dodging and countering.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Katana | Four hits: cut, X-cut, lunging stab, and a quick-draw that dashes through and cuts twice |
| Hold click | Iai Dash | Hand on the hilt, she flash-steps through everything ahead, untouchable, and the cuts open a beat later in a burst of petals. Half a charge, half the dash |
| Q (20 energy) | Sakura Wave | A flying crescent slash that passes through enemies and cuts their shots out of the air |
| E (30 energy) | Kamae | A counter stance. If anything hits her in it, she flash-steps to the attacker, cuts everything around it, and her next hit does triple damage |
| R (ultimate) | Issen | Time stops while she flickers from enemy to enemy in 11 cuts, then she sheathes her sword and everything she cut bursts |

## Kill and death
- **Her kills:** a burst of petals, and the cut line hangs in the air.
- **Her death:** a storm of cherry blossoms rises around her and thickens until she fades behind it, then she scatters into petals and the wind carries them away. *She scatters with the blossoms.*
