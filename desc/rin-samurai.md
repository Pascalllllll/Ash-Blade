# Rin, the Samurai

**Unlock:** make 25 perfect dodges in one run · **Awakened form:** Oni Rin

The rules every fighter shares (controls, charged attacks, Flash Strike, awakening and its bonuses) are in [heroes.md](heroes.md).

> The last samurai of the Red Oni clan. Still has homework due Friday.

## Look
Bright cyan hair fading to pink at the tips, tied with pink ribbons, big violet eyes, a white sailor top with a red ribbon, a red sash and a navy skirt. Her katana cuts in pink arcs and cherry petals, and pink afterimages trail behind her when she runs.

Awakened as **Oni Rin**, her hair turns violet, her reds and pinks turn hot pink and her skirt deep purple. Her sword arcs, cuts and petals turn gold and draw much thicker.

## How she plays
The fastest fighter, with two dashes. Her reach is short, so she fights up close and sends enemy shots back at them.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 4 / 5 | 5 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 2 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Katana | Four hits: cut, X-cut, lunging stab, and a quick-draw that dashes through and cuts twice |
| Dash, then attack | Flash Strike | Dash into Senko for her biggest hit |
| Hold click | Iai Dash | Hand on the hilt, she flash-steps through everything ahead, untouchable, and the cuts open a beat later in a burst of petals. Every shot on her path is parried back |
| Q (50 energy) | Senko | She slashes and a beam of light shoots out of the blade across the arena in an instant: huge damage to every enemy on the line, every enemy shot on it destroyed, and the recoil pushes her back a step |
| E (30 energy) | Hana Arashi | For 4 seconds a ring of petal blades spins around her. It cuts anything that comes close, parries every shot that reaches it, and she moves 25% faster |
| R (ultimate) | Issen | Time stops while she flickers from enemy to enemy in 11 cuts, then she sheathes her sword and everything she cut bursts |

**Awakened powers:** a petal cut opens on every enemy a beat after her blade, her skills cut a ring that parries every shot near her, and after Issen the blossom storm rises around her for free. Her charged Iai Dash opens a second, wider cut along her path a moment later, ending in a burst of petals.

## Kill and death
- **Her kills:** a burst of petals, and the cut line hangs in the air.
- **Her death:** a storm of cherry blossoms rises around her and thickens until she fades behind it, then she scatters into petals and the wind carries them away. *She scatters with the blossoms.*
