# Kael, the Blade

**Unlock:** free from the start · **Awakened form:** Ashen Kael

The rules every fighter shares (controls, charged attacks, Flash Strike, awakening and its bonuses) are in [heroes.md](heroes.md).

> The last guard of the pit. His blade holds the final ember of the old sun.

## Look
Spiky violet hair over dark, determined eyes, an ember-orange scarf wrapped high, and a dark violet tunic with a gold belt buckle. His Ember Blade glows in every swing, leaving a burning arc.

Awakened as **Ashen Kael**, his hair and clothes turn silver-grey and his scarf turns gold.

## How he plays
The all-rounder and the first fighter you get. Quick on his feet, solid in a fight, short on reach.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 3 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Sword Combo | Four hits: slash, X-cut, thrust, whirlwind |
| Hold click | Ashen Crescent | A wide burning cut that also throws a wave of fire ahead |
| Q (30 energy) | Rift | Blinks forward and cuts every enemy on the line |
| E (45 energy) | Tempest | Knocks enemies back and chains lightning between them |
| R (ultimate) | Thousand Cuts | Stops time and cuts every enemy on screen |

**Awakened powers:** his cuts set enemies on fire, his skills throw a ring of fire, and Thousand Cuts leaves the whole screen burning. His Ashen Crescent fans out four more burning crescents, and a line of white fire erupts along the cut.

## Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies catch fire, and embers and ash rise off the body.
- **His death:** he drops to one knee, plants his sword and crumbles to ash from the head down. The sword stays, its flame going out. *The last ember goes out.*
