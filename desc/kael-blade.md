# Kael, the Blade

**Class:** Blade · **Unlock:** you start with him · **Awakened form:** Ashen Kael

> The last guard of the pit. His blade holds the final ember of the old sun.

## Look
Spiky dark violet hair and an ember-orange scarf pulled up over his mouth. A bone-white shirt under a grey-violet tunic, dark trousers and boots, and a longsword whose edge still glows with the old sun's fire. The scarf streams behind him in the wind.

Awakened as **Ashen Kael**, his hair burns white, his scarf turns gold and his clothes go a deep blood red.

Awakened, his cuts set enemies on fire, his powers throw a ring of fire, and Thousand Cuts leaves the whole screen burning. Awakening also makes basic hits and the ultimate 30% stronger, Q and E a quarter cheaper and a fifth faster to recharge, and the ultimate charge a quarter faster, keeping 30% of its charge after each use. His charged Ashen Crescent throws three crescents instead of one, the middle one white flame.

## How he plays
The all-rounder and the first fighter you get. Quick on his feet, solid in a fight, short on reach.

| Health | Power | Speed | Range | Toughness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 3 / 5 | 4 / 5 | 2 / 5 | 3 / 5 |

| Input | Move | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Click | Sword Combo | Four hits: slash, X-cut, thrust, whirlwind |
| Hold click | Ashen Crescent | A wide burning cut that also throws a wave of fire ahead. Half a charge, half the cut |
| Q (30 energy) | Rift | Blinks forward and cuts every enemy on the line |
| E (45 energy) | Tempest | Knocks enemies back and chains lightning between them |
| R (ultimate) | Thousand Cuts | Stops time and cuts every enemy on screen |

## Kill and death
- **His kills:** enemies catch fire, and embers and ash rise off the body.
- **His death:** he drops to one knee, plants his sword and crumbles to ash from the head down. The sword stays, its flame going out. *The last ember goes out.*
